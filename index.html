About

The founder
behind the label.

WAHAB INNOVATIONS started with a simple idea. A young man should be able to dress sharp without looking like everyone else.

Every piece is designed in-house and cut with intent, made to move between the studio, the street, and the moments that matter. What began as a personal obsession with fit and finish grew into a label with a point of view.

Services

What we make.

S/01

Bespoke Suits

A full paper pattern drafted to your measure, hand-cut and canvassed. Two-piece, three-piece, or dinner suit.

S/02

Made to Measure

Our house blocks refined to your dimensions. The bespoke feel, delivered in four weeks rather than twelve.

S/03

Shirts & Formalwear

Hand-finished collars, mother-of-pearl buttons, single-needle seams. Cut for evening or every day.

Bridal & Wedding

Coordinated tailoring for the party, timed to the day and remembered long after it.

S/06

The Travelling Tailor
